# Code of Conduct

## Our promise

We want Tethercam to be a friendly place for everyone, whatever their age, body, ability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual orientation.

## Do

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are new to open source.
- Respect different opinions and experience.
- Give and accept helpful feedback.
- Say sorry when you make a mistake, and learn from it.

## Do not

- Use sexual language or images, or make unwanted advances.
- Insult, troll or attack people.
- Harass people, in public or in private.
- Publish private information about other people, such as an address or email, without their permission.
- Do other things that a reasonable person would think are not acceptable here.

## Where this applies

In this repository: issues, pull requests, discussions and code reviews. It also applies when you represent the project in other places.

## Report a problem

Report the content with GitHub's **Report content** option (the `...` menu on a comment), and mention @kirawins17 or the maintainers in a private way if you can.
The maintainers will read every report and keep the reporter's name private.

## What happens next

The maintainers can edit or delete comments, close discussions, and block people from the project for a time or for always. They will choose the action that fits the problem.
