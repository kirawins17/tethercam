# Get help

1. Read **[Problems?](README.md#problems)** in the README. It has the most common fixes.
2. Search [issues](https://github.com/kirawins17/tethercam/issues?q=is%3Aissue) and [discussions](https://github.com/kirawins17/tethercam/discussions). Someone may have had the same problem.
3. Still stuck? [Ask in Discussions](https://github.com/kirawins17/tethercam/discussions/new?category=q-a). Tell us your phone, your PC system, and the text in the Tethercam window.
4. Found a bug? [Open a bug report](https://github.com/kirawins17/tethercam/issues/new/choose).
5. Found a security problem? Do not post it in public. Read [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Tethercam is made by volunteers. We try to answer in 1 to 2 days.
